## Key Technical Decisions (for reference)

- `StyleSheet.absoluteFill` (not `.absoluteFillObject`) for QR viewfinder overlay
- BLE mock uses cumulative `setTimeout` offsets (1500/2500/3500 ms)
- `rideHistory` stored as in-memory array in `rideService` — acceptable for mock scope
- `AccountScreen` typed as `StackScreenProps<AccountStackParamList, 'AccountMain'>`
- `textOnPrimary = #0F0F0F` (black) — Electric Green (#C6FF2D) is too light for text
- `tailwind.config.js`: DS colors must be inlined — cannot `require('./src/theme/theme')` (ESM/jiti issue)
