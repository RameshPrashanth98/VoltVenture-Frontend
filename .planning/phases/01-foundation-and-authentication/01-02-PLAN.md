<objective>
Build the complete onboarding vertical slice: Splash screen (brand reveal) → 3-slide feature onboarding → Auth Landing (primary CTA to sign up). A first-time user who opens the app can experience the full pre-auth journey before reaching the Sign Up or Log In screens.

Purpose: Establishes the brand's first impression and filters tourist intent — tourists who engage with onboarding understand the app's core value before creating an account.

Output: Three fully functional screens implementing D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04 interaction contracts, all copy from UI-SPEC.md, and correct DS token styling.
</objective>
