## Phase Goal

**As a** tourist, **I want to** tap Reserve on a bike and see a booking confirmation with a countdown timer, **so that** I know my bike is held and I can proceed to unlock it.

<objective>
Deliver the end-to-end vertical slice from "tap Reserve" to "see BookingConfirmation with countdown timer and unlock CTAs". This plan wires the modal navigation stack, creates the booking service, and builds the full BookingConfirmation screen. The three unlock screens (QRScanner, BLEUnlock, UnlockSuccess) are created as stubs — implemented fully in Plans 03-02 and 03-03.

Purpose: Establish the BookingStack modal foundation and deliver a working, navigable BookingConfirmation screen. After this plan, a user can tap Reserve → see the booking confirmation with countdown — the most critical moment in the booking flow.

Output: bookingService.ts, Booking type, BookingStackParamList, BookingNavigator, updated RootNavigator, BookingConfirmationScreen (full), three stub screens, MapScreen onReserve handler wired.
</objective>
